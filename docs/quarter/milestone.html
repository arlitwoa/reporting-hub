Assessments & Grading in Aohere

Description

Deliver assessment and grading capabilities through Aohere, enabling the capture, validation, transfer, and management of learner results while reducing dependence on legacy systems and supporting a streamlined, auditable academic records process. The Assessments & Grading milestone focuses on moving assessment and grade management capabilities into Aohere as part of the wider replacement and decommissioning of legacy student administration processes and supporting systems. • Assessment and grading information becomes managed through the future-state ecosystem centred on Aohere, reducing reliance on legacy grade-processing mechanisms. • Support for grade extraction from learning platforms such as iAkoranga (Moodle), including validation that all required assessments have been completed before a grade is transferred. • Automated grade transfer processes and grade-readiness checks to ensure assessment outcomes are complete and accurate before academic results are recorded. • Introduction of automated grade-locking and outcome processing to improve consistency, reduce manual effort, and support compliance reporting requirements. • Establishment of a future-state foundation for academic completion, conferment, reporting, and learner record management as legacy capabilities are progressively decoupled.
